# Slice

## Purpose

Pick and shape the next piece of work in a flow cadence.

## Steps

1. Take the top item from ⟨tracker⟩ or the user's request.
2. Cut it to the smallest change that delivers observable value.
3. Write acceptance criteria as observable behavior.

## Done when

- One slice with acceptance criteria is ready.
